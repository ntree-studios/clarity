# Looking after a road bicycle

Last weekend I took my bicycle out on a long ride through the hills. The chain skipped on the steep climbs, so I stopped to check the gears and the derailleur cable before the descent.

A bicycle chain needs cleaning and oil every few hundred miles of riding. A dirty chain wears the gears and the cassette, and the shifting gets rough on every climb and every long ride through the hills.

Brake pads on a road bicycle wear quickly on wet descents. Check the pads before a long ride, replace them when the grooves are gone, and adjust the brake cable so the lever pulls firmly on the descent.

Tire pressure changes how a bicycle rides on rough roads. Lower pressure gives more grip on the descent, while higher pressure rolls faster on smooth climbs. Check the tires and the chain before every long ride.
